## How the senses and decoder were reached (also in ADR-0028)

1. First headless run: the fly walked a loop, crossed bitter, jumped at the shadow, but missed the food. It has no way to sense food at a distance.
2. Start moved 4 mm east so its path crosses food: it fed, but feeding flickered about 40 times (MN9 near a single threshold), and the 5 mm hop stayed inside the food.
3. Feeding hysteresis (30 Hz on, 15 Hz off) and a 15 mm hop. Seed 0: onto food 1.23 s, feeds, shadow → jump and leaves food 6.11 s. Seed 1: onto food 1.10 s, feeds (one 9 ms break), shadow → jump 6.11 s.

## Phase 3 gate: the builder's assessment

- **Shown:** walking; stops and feeds on food; flees a looming shadow (even mid-meal); turning bias from the brain; bitter suppression measured separately. Determinism: 1057/1057 at the gate.
