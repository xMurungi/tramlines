  - **Full null brains do nothing the body can see:** never move (15/15), every readout 0 Hz. Real wiring walks, feeds, refuses bitter and escapes (3/3).
  - **Why:** real wiring converges each sense onto few neurons (sugar: 289 receivers against about 1,520 in a null brain).
  - **Decoder-free:** null brains still carry readable sense signals (10–12/12), but they stop about one synapse in; real wiring carries them hundreds of neurons deep and into the movement neurons.
